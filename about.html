About Emlalatini Development Centre

Providing second chances through flexible education

🏛️ Our Mandate

Emlalatini Development Centre is a government institution offering Open and Distance Learning (ODL) programmes. We provide first and second opportunities to youth and adults who could not complete their secondary education.

👁️ Vision

To be a leading Open and Distance Learning provider in the region.

🎯 Mission

To provide access to quality and affordable education through flexible and inclusive learning systems.

📜 Our Journey

Foundation

Established to provide second-chance education opportunities.

Expansion

Introduced JC and EGCSE programmes across multiple centres.

Today

Recognised as a trusted examination centre in Eswatini.

👥 Who We Serve

Adults returning to education
Students upgrading results
Career changers
Working learners
Flexible subject learners
JC repeat candidates
